The six conditions that fail release validation

Role and Release Matrix v1 section 6 names them, and any one of them is enough:

  • Any visible recommendation lacks provenance.
  • Any visible clinical value lacks a unit.
  • Any visible item is not present in the reviewed clinical plan.
  • Any draft or raw AI field is included.
  • Any required hold is unresolved.
  • Physician actor or signature semantics are missing.

The second condition is the reason every value in the preview above carries its unit. It is the same P4 rule the instrument follows everywhere else, promoted here into a release gate.

Why three acts instead of one Sign and release

Role and Release Matrix v1 section 3 lists Sign plan, Create lab order request and Release plan as three separate permissions with three different rows, and one of them (Release plan) carries a delegation clause the other two do not. Collapsing them into one control would make the surface claim an authority model the backend does not have.

It also matters clinically. An order can transmit before the note is locked, and a note can be locked with orders still pending; both are legitimate and both happen. A single button forces one ordering onto every case and silently performs two acts the physician only consented to one of.

The rail above numbers four steps and the matrix numbers three acts, so the two do not line up and the act headings carry no ordinal. Step 1 is the plan and is not an act at all. Step 2 is matrix act 1, step 3 is matrix act 2, step 4 is matrix act 3. Steps 2 and 3 have no order between them; step 4 needs both, in either order.
