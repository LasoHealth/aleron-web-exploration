Baseline findings

finding The checked checkbox is filled with --signal-forward. Gold is the surface’s one signal and it is spent here on six selection boxes, so the page has no gold left for anything that matters and the selection reads as decoration.

finding Third navigation mechanism on one page: a segmented section strip, an underline page nav, and a two-step wizard rail, stacked in 100px of vertical space. The page nav also carries a practice-level link out of the patient chart, so patient scope and practice scope share one strip.

finding Card titles are the plan text, not the action title. The engine derives a short title and it is used in the step 2 manifest and nowhere here, so the same action is named two different ways on two consecutive screens.

finding Severity is carried three times over on each card and never with a legend: a 3px left rail, the colour of the urgency word, and the word itself. Tier 1 pins show Tier 1 flag where ranked candidates show High or Standard, so the two vocabularies sit in the same column.

finding The dock’s domain line prints a.domain raw and uppercased, so a guideline syndrome name and the internal key CVD appear in the same list. Everywhere else on the card domainLabel() expands it to Preventive Cardiovascular (CVD).

finding The rule that mandatory entries cannot be rejected is stated only inside a popover behind a 16px lowercase i, and the popover then contradicts it: every item, including these, is yours to include or drop. The pins are pre-checked and look identical to the ranked cards apart from a surface tint.

finding Order chips are the audit face in tracked caps and carry no unit, no quantity and no indication of which are transmittable. Two of the six actions produce no chips at all, which is indistinguishable from an action whose orders were removed.

note Engine score is printed as a bare number (ENG #1 · 8.42) with no scale, no unit and no interval. It is the only quantitative ranking signal on the page and it is unreadable without the engine source.

note This whole route runs a second class vocabulary (#cp) with its own type ramp, its own card, its own buttons and its own tab strip, ported wholesale from a prototype. Nothing here uses .amd-card, and the footnote still calls the system v0.4.
