Baseline findings

finding Group C is labelled Metabolic & glycemic by the engine but carries the genetic group’s prose (fixed architecture, dominant lesion wins) and the genetic group’s MAX rule, while group D is Genetic & familial and carries the behavioural prose. groupRule() and groupDescription() in portal.js are one letter out of register with app/Services/CVD/Domain/. The arithmetic on this page is therefore wrong: three metabolic terms that should multiply are being collapsed with MAX.

finding The formula and coefficient rows run the audit face at caption size across the full measure with word-break: break-word. That is 480 characters of unbreakable notation in the retired face, and it is the tallest element on the page.

finding Numbers appear at four precisions with no stated convention: the headline is 16%, the baseline block is 12.65%, the log-HR is three decimals and the multipliers are two. Nothing on the page says which is the reportable figure.

finding Effect direction is carried by an arrow glyph plus a colour that only separates crit from everything else. A modifier of 1.12× and a modifier of 1.00× both render in secondary ink, so a real amplification and a no-op look the same until you read the digits.

finding The header card metadata blocks are the audit face and are the only place the pre-modifier baseline appears. There is no unit on +0.231 log-HR beyond the word itself and no interval anywhere on the page.

note The Engine row the panel can emit never renders for this domain: step1Rows() makes it the fallback for domains with no identified base model, so CVD, CKD and Neuro show Base model instead and Cancer and Metabolic show Engine. The same table means two different things across the five tabs.

note Six sections, all flat cards, all the same weight, in a fixed order that never collapses. The physician scrolls past the entire derivation to reach the information gaps, which is the only part of the page that names an action.
