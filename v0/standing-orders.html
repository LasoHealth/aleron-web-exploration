Model under review The Canvas clinical-documentation ADR (§5) recommends genetics move from standing-order authorization to an individual-physician order with a pre-test counseling acknowledgment. The physician-portal ordering flow enforces that acknowledgment today; the mobile standing-order rail does not yet.

Baseline findings

finding Nothing on this route is patient-specific. It sits behind the patient identity block and a chart tab strip, but the two cards read environment configuration and render identically for every patient in the cohort. It is a practice-level artifact filed under a patient.

finding Two Tier-1 cards on one screen. The design system budgets one elevated holder per surface, and this page also stacks a page-level hazard annunciator, a card-level hazard annunciator and a card-level advisory inside them.

finding The genetics card says three contradictory things at once: the header pill says Not configured, the record table says ordering is blocked, and the authorized menu below lists two panels as Orderable. Orderable there means the catalog has a test id, not that anyone can order it.

finding The shipped copy uses an em dash as a sentence connector in six places on this route (Read only, Not set twice, Disabled, the fail-closed rollup, the unconfigured-card body). The design system forbids the em dash in product copy outright, so the replica substitutes a colon and records the divergence here.

finding The rollup annunciator prints a PHP class reference, \App\Services\Junction\StandingOrder::for(), to a physician. So does the empty state on Care Plan, which prints an artisan command.

note The advisory admits that the portal and the mobile rail enforce different gates for the same genetic order and leaves it there. There is no owner, no date and no control, so the divergence is documented to the physician rather than to anyone who could close it.

note Read only is stated in the subtitle and then never reinforced. The tables look exactly like the editable tables on Lab Orders, down to the pill vocabulary, so the surface gives no visual cue that nothing here can be acted on.
