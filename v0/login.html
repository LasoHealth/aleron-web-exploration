Baseline findings

finding The page’s only heading is --size-h3, one step below the h2 every signed-in page gives its patient name. The sign-in screen is the smallest-titled screen in the product.

finding The guest layout re-applies the persisted meridian-register value before first paint but ships no register toggle, so a physician who left the portal in flight deck signs in through a navy card and cannot switch back until after authentication.

finding The form is novalidate and there is no inline field validation. Every failure, including a mistyped email, comes back as one hazard annunciator above the fields after a round trip, and the annunciator carries the server string verbatim.

note Two different primary controls sit at the same place in the flow: an anchor styled as a button that leaves the product, and a submit button that posts to it. Nothing on the SSO path says where the physician is being sent until Entra loads.

note The gold wordmark bar is the single piece of brand ornament in the whole product, and this is the only screen where it is not competing with a patient name.
