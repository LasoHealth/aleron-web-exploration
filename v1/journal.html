Notes for review

fixed There was no way to log out, and no way to tell who was logged in. .prail__patient names the patient, so across 21 screens nothing said whose session this was and nothing ended it. v0 has both in its header, Dr. A. Okafor beside a Log out button, and the proposals dropped them when the header became a rail. A clinical surface that cannot say who is signed in cannot be handed to the next physician at the same workstation, which is the ordinary case in a clinic. The rail foot now carries the actor and the way out, under a rule, at the bottom where the least frequent act belongs. Proposed in section B as a domain.chart-rail addition, using the foot's existing exemption for a non-section action rather than claiming a new one.

fixed As bare underlined text the control was 15px tall, for the one act that ends the session. A text link is the sanctioned shape where a filled control will not fit, per register O5's fallback, but the hit area still has to be real: the label keeps its size and the target grew to 32px, with the same pointer: coarse guard the row-density variant uses restoring the full 44px floor on touch. Measured 14.35:1 flight-deck and 5.34:1 day.

new The whole screen. v0 has no journal at all: notes live in Canvas behind the EMR deep link, so a physician cannot see the chronological record without leaving Aleron.

new The spine's node vocabulary is shape, not tint: filled ink circle signed, open gold ring draft, open square imported. An external document is a different kind of thing from an unsigned draft, and P2 puts category on shape so the difference survives the register flip and a grayscale print. The one open gold ring is this screen's gold.

new The orders table header says Status · live from the ledger. The signed text never changes but the order statuses beneath it do, and the header is the only thing telling the reader which half is frozen.

gap The spine is screen-local (.jr-list::before plus .jr-node). components.css has .amd-milestones / .amd-stepper, but those model progress through a known sequence with a segment between steps. A journal is an open record with no final step and three node kinds, so the stepper is the wrong pattern rather than a restylable one. Candidate for PROPOSED domain.record-spine if v2 needs it twice.

gap Order status words are advisory (proposed) and baseline (required), never tiles. A proposed order is not a hazard. But the reference surface also emits canceled and rejected · suppressed next cycle with no declared treatment, and those are the two a physician most needs to spot. Declare them.
