Vitality

The felt-experience model: four patient-reported outcomes, recovery context, and the drivers that may move them.

Outcome measures

Each outcome gets its own graph. At intake these are one-point patient-reported measures, then they trend forward.

Energy

0-10 self-report · Felt energy, vigor, fatigue burden.

Low 4of 10

Energy is the patient priority when low. It is an outcome, not a presumed cause. Next: repeat PRO at 30d, 90d, and 180d.

Mood

0-10 self-report · Emotional tone, positive affect, lift.

In range 6of 10

Mood is part of the outcome profile. If it is stable, it should not dominate the plan by default. Next: repeat PRO with PHQ/GAD if symptoms rise.

Body ease

0-10 self-report · Physical ease, pain burden, readiness to move.

Borderline 5of 10

Body ease shows whether the plan makes movement and training feel more possible. Next: repeat PRO with pain screen if still low.

Clarity

0-10 self-report · Perceived sharpness, attention, brain fog.

Low 4of 10

Clarity should be interpreted alongside HRV and drivers, but it is not itself the cause. Next: repeat PRO and compare against recovery context.

Notes for review

fixed There was no way to log out, and no way to tell who was logged in. .prail__patient names the patient, so across 21 screens nothing said whose session this was and nothing ended it. v0 has both in its header, Dr. A. Okafor beside a Log out button, and the proposals dropped them when the header became a rail. A clinical surface that cannot say who is signed in cannot be handed to the next physician at the same workstation, which is the ordinary case in a clinic. The rail foot now carries the actor and the way out, under a rule, at the bottom where the least frequent act belongs. Proposed in section B as a domain.chart-rail addition, using the foot's existing exemption for a non-section action rather than claiming a new one.

fixed As bare underlined text the control was 15px tall, for the one act that ends the session. A text link is the sanctioned shape where a filled control will not fit, per register O5's fallback, but the hit area still has to be real: the label keeps its size and the target grew to 32px, with the same pointer: coarse guard the row-density variant uses restoring the full 44px floor on touch. Measured 14.35:1 flight-deck and 5.34:1 day.

new The whole screen. v0 collects no patient-reported outcome anywhere, so the switcher marks v0 absent. The fixed eight-slot x-scale is the load-bearing decision: four slots are in the future and stay visibly empty rather than the chart rescaling to its one point and implying a trend.

new Low outcome scores are advisory words, not ember tiles. A 4 of 10 on energy is a finding to interpret, not a hazard, and P3 reserves the filled tile so that real hazards outrank quiet advisories by construction.

new The gold on this screen is the desired-range band, which P6 names explicitly as furniture ("reference bands ... draw from ink alphas and the gold wash through tokens"). It is drawn from --p-goldwash, a color-mix off --signal-forward, so it can never drift from the gold and the register flip carries it.

gap PROPOSED token.instrument-furniture added --p-goldwash to section B of physician-chrome.css. P6 names the gold wash but tokens.css v0.5.0 does not declare it, so every surface that needs a reference band has been inventing its own alpha.

gap The Actions tab is disabled here. The reference surface's vitality action plane plots the same levers on a second, non-QALY scale, and its own caption warns "it is not a new score and it does not replace the QALY action map". Two scales for one decision is the thing the action map exists to prevent. Resolve before v2.
