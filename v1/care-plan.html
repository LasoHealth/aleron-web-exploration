Notes for review

fixed There was no way to log out, and no way to tell who was logged in. .prail__patient names the patient, so across 21 screens nothing said whose session this was and nothing ended it. v0 has both in its header, Dr. A. Okafor beside a Log out button, and the proposals dropped them when the header became a rail. A clinical surface that cannot say who is signed in cannot be handed to the next physician at the same workstation, which is the ordinary case in a clinic. The rail foot now carries the actor and the way out, under a rule, at the bottom where the least frequent act belongs. Proposed in section B as a domain.chart-rail addition, using the foot's existing exemption for a non-section action rather than claiming a new one.

fixed As bare underlined text the control was 15px tall, for the one act that ends the session. A text link is the sanctioned shape where a filled control will not fit, per register O5's fallback, but the hit area still has to be real: the label keeps its size and the target grew to 32px, with the same pointer: coarse guard the row-density variant uses restoring the full 44px floor on touch. Measured 14.35:1 flight-deck and 5.34:1 day.

finding Linked levers renders two raw identifiers, breast_mri_screening and pancreatic_surveillance_if_family_history, on the product surface. They are library keys, not clinical language, and a physician acts on neither. Left as it reads, because v1 recreates the ratified reference surface rather than correcting it; this is a finding the reference would need to absorb.

finding tracked as a Goal implies a write the Canvas FHIR API does not offer: Goal is read and search only, and a Goal is created by the Goals command. Left as it reads, because v1 recreates the ratified reference surface rather than correcting it. v2 and v3 carry the correction; this is the finding the reference surface would need to absorb.

new The bounded document. v0's care plan is a form; this is a note with an edge, and the edge is the whole claim: everything inside it is what the signature covers, everything in the right rail is why, and none of the rail enters the record.

new The commitment sentence is computed, not written. Four problems, one added to the active list, one updating an existing problem, two note-only, six orders released. A physician signing a note should be able to read what the signature will do without opening anything.

gap Sign & release is disabled and says so. That is the finding, not the placeholder. The reference surface ships this control permanently disabled with cursor: not-allowed and no explanation, on a screen whose whole subject is the signature. Three separate acts are collapsed into one label: signing the note, authorizing the orders, and releasing the plan to the patient. BRIEF.md's own terminology treats them as separate acts. v2 has to split them and wire each one, or the surface is naming an act it cannot perform.

gap .ap-doc and the basis rail are screen-local. They are a strong candidate for PROPOSED domain.document once care-plan-release exists in v2 and there are two consumers, but one instance is not a component.

new The selected order takes the gold wash and the gold left rail: the screen's one gold, spent on selection focus per P2. The required-channel card underneath is deliberately not gold, because an obligation is not a selection.
