What the agent cannot do
No orders
AI contributes candidate generation, not free-text orders. The final plan is generated only after candidates are scored and placed on the map.
No other patient
The context strip above is the whole scope. There is no cohort view, no cross-patient comparison, and no memory between charts.
No unscored value
A signal that has not been structured and scored has no number, and the agent will say so rather than estimating one.
Every turn
Closes with the same provenance line. A turn that cannot cite it is a turn that should not have been emitted.

Answers are limited to the selected patient, current model outputs, the action library, genetics, candidate signals, and the map.
