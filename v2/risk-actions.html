What changed, and where it came from

fixed There was no way to log out, and no way to tell who was logged in. .prail__patient names the patient, so across 21 screens nothing said whose session this was and nothing ended it. v0 has both in its header, Dr. A. Okafor beside a Log out button, and the proposals dropped them when the header became a rail. A clinical surface that cannot say who is signed in cannot be handed to the next physician at the same workstation, which is the ordinary case in a clinic. The rail foot now carries the actor and the way out, under a rule, at the bottom where the least frequent act belongs. Proposed in section B as a domain.chart-rail addition, using the foot's existing exemption for a non-section action rather than claiming a new one.

fixed As bare underlined text the control was 15px tall, for the one act that ends the session. A text link is the sanctioned shape where a filled control will not fit, per register O5's fallback, but the hit area still has to be real: the label keeps its size and the target grew to 32px, with the same pointer: coarse guard the row-density variant uses restoring the full 44px floor on touch. Measured 14.35:1 flight-deck and 5.34:1 day.

fixed .ax-send .amd-button restyled a canonical component. It also forked the value it was duplicating: the local rule set --size-caption where the chrome's proposed data-density="row" variant sets --size-small, so the same dense row action was two sizes depending on which screen you were on. The 21 buttons now carry the attribute and take the proposed variant, leaving one definition instead of two that disagree.

new The action map, the evidence ledger, the AI candidate pipeline and the action-changing gates are the reference surface's Actions pane, rebuilt to the same specification: viewBox 0 0 940 560, signed patient-QALY ticks, a continuous 0 to 1 confidence axis, the value frame stated top right, and the net-harmful zone below zero. v0 has no equivalent at any fidelity.

carried Send to care plan on every ledger row. v0's Care Plan at v0/care-plan.html lets a physician select engine-ranked actions inside the note composer, which is the only place v0 connects scoring to a plan. In v1 the map and the plan are not connected at all: the ledger prices a lever and then the physician retypes it. The affordance is refused on a net-harmful row, because overriding a negative score is a documented decision and not a one-click send.

new Category rides on shape, per P2: a circle acts, a square measures, dashed and open is gated, an indigo dashed square is an AI-scored candidate. Drug, lifestyle and procedure are not hues; the arm lives in the ledger row's provenance line.

new The gold budget is spent once, on selection: the ring around bariatric metabolic surgery and the dashed tether to the OGTT that would unlock it. Nothing else on the screen is gold.

gap The VO₂max ladder has no colour of its own. The reference surface spends a sixth hue on it; the ratified palette declares five signals and none of them is the fitness course. The ladder here borrows ember at stroke weight while the net-harmful zone uses ember as a wash, which is two meanings on one signal and is a defect, not a solution. The register needs either a sixth signal or a shape-only ladder.

gap .amd-button's 44 px minimum height is right for a page-level act and wrong for one repeated on fifteen ledger rows. The local override shrinks it to 32 px, which is a restyle of a canonical component and therefore a finding: the registry needs a dense row-action variant.

gap Screening has no canonical icon. The magnifier with a plus is drawn inline in the canonical stroke grammar and flagged provisional in the markup.

dropped v0's criticality ranking of recommendations (Critical / High / Routine) as the ordering principle. The map already prices every lever on one continuous scale, so a three-word band over the top of it is a second, coarser ranking that can disagree with the first.

dropped The reference surface's hover tooltip div. Tooltip-only content is unreachable by keyboard and invisible in print; every mark carries an SVG <title> instead, and the ledger below carries the same content as readable rows.
