What changed, and where it came from

fixed There was no way to log out, and no way to tell who was logged in. .prail__patient names the patient, so across 21 screens nothing said whose session this was and nothing ended it. v0 has both in its header, Dr. A. Okafor beside a Log out button, and the proposals dropped them when the header became a rail. A clinical surface that cannot say who is signed in cannot be handed to the next physician at the same workstation, which is the ordinary case in a clinic. The rail foot now carries the actor and the way out, under a rule, at the bottom where the least frequent act belongs. Proposed in section B as a domain.chart-rail addition, using the foot's existing exemption for a non-section action rather than claiming a new one.

fixed As bare underlined text the control was 15px tall, for the one act that ends the session. A text link is the sanctioned shape where a filled control will not fit, per register O5's fallback, but the hit area still has to be real: the label keeps its size and the target grew to 32px, with the same pointer: coarse guard the row-density variant uses restoring the full 44px floor on touch. Measured 14.35:1 flight-deck and 5.34:1 day.

new The whole screen. PRODUCTION_PATIENT_PHYSICIAN_STRATEGY.md lists Patient message action last in its minimum staging physician surface, and neither v0 nor the reference surface has any physician-to-patient channel.

new The release gate on the composer. The reference picker offers only the four items this chart has released, and the withheld categories are printed with the reason rather than silently absent. A gate you cannot see is a gate you will argue with.

new Physician identity on every physician-originated message: name, role, organisation and NPI. A patient acting on a clinical instruction is entitled to know which licensed human issued it, and the matrix requires actor attribution on the stored record anyway.

gap No canonical message-thread component exists. interaction.ai-thread (.amd-ai-thread) is the model channel and deliberately not reused here, because a physician message and an AI message have different provenance obligations. The thread layout is screen-local until a registry entry exists.

gap The withheld-content list reuses .preason, proposed in physician-chrome.css section B for readiness blockers. Same shape, same job: state what is withheld and why, never grey something out and leave the physician guessing.

dropped Any unread badge on the thread rows. The rail already carries the count, and a second unread treatment inside the list would spend attention on recency when the thread list's only job is to get you back to a conversation.
