Version 2

The reference surface, plus everything the shipped portal can already do

Sixteen screens in two families. v1 recreated the canonical reference surface and stopped where it stops: at a disabled Sign and release button, inside one patient's chart, with no way in and no way out. v2 keeps every one of those screens and adds the four things a working portal needs around them, plus the five capabilities v0 has today and v1 has no home for.

What v2 adds over v1

  • A way in. v1 opens on a chart. v2 opens on a queue, ordered by lifecycle state, so the first question a physician answers is which patient rather than which tab.
  • The release act, in full. v1's care plan ends at Sign and release, not wired. v2 replaces it with the three separate acts the role matrix actually defines: note lock, order authorization, patient release, each with its own attestation.
  • An audit read. The matrix grants the physician Audit log: read relevant patient. Neither prior version has a surface where that permission can be exercised.
  • A patient channel. The production strategy names patient message action in its minimum surface. v1 has Aleron AI, which is a channel to the model, not to the patient.
  • Screening. A seventh chart section for the obligations that are not ranked against opportunities, which in v1 are scattered between the action map's required channel and the care plan's preventive gaps.

v0 capabilities absorbed

  • The cohort list from v0/patients.html, including its six-domain risk read, which becomes a density toggle on Panel.
  • Ordering from v0/lab-orders.html: the authorize-order card, the collection methods, the genetics counseling gate, the orders table and the review-and-sign flow. v1 proposes order intents and has nowhere to place one.
  • Order authorization records from v0/standing-orders.html: both standing orders, their fail-closed behaviour and the genetics advisory.
  • The Canvas door from v0/emr.html, kept almost verbatim, with a reconciliation panel added beneath it.
  • The note composer from v0/care-plan.html: the indication field, per-problem assessment and plan, the auto-drafted narrative and the add-to-problem-list toggle, folded into the reference surface's workbench.

The release path · drawn as a sequence, because it is one

Signing the note, authorizing the orders and releasing to the patient are three acts, not three steps of one act. The matrix gives each its own permission row, and only the third one is delegable. Everything before act 1 is reversible; nothing after act 3 is.

The blocked path forks at act 3. When any of the four gating checks fails, the lifecycle state is Signature blocked, the act buttons are absent rather than greyed, and the four named blockers are the screen. That specimen is on Care plan release.

Where the state words come from

Every lifecycle string on every v2 screen is a literal from Aleron MD Role and Release Matrix v1 section 4, rendered in sentence case and never invented: physician review pending, plan editing, plan ready for signature, signature blocked, plan signed, plan released, patient plan available, action tracking active, monitoring update requires review, physician hold, results partially received. Section 4's Release allowed column is the reason act 3 is reachable only from plan signed.

What changed, and where it came from

new Two families rather than one. v0 has a header nav and a per-chart tab strip; v1 has one rail and one patient. v2 declares two fixed rails on the same .prail anatomy, which is what register O4 means by rails being fixed per surface family.

new The release path is drawn as an ordered sequence with three separate acts, not as one signature. This is the single most important thing the reference surface teaches and the thing v0 gets half right: v0 separates signing orders from locking the note and then has no third act.

carried Five v0 capabilities, each landing on a named screen and each annotated on that screen with the v0 file it came from. The gold left edge on a node is the marker.

gap Screening has no rail icon and Lab orders, Standing orders and EMR have no rail item at all. They are reached from the care plan, which works but understates them: three of the four carried v0 capabilities are currently second-class in the navigation.

dropped v0's login screen has no v2 counterpart in this set. Real physician login is on the production strategy's list, but it is an auth surface rather than a workflow surface and nothing in the role matrix shapes it, so it is left in v0 as the baseline.
