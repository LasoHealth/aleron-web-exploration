Model under review The Canvas clinical-documentation ADR section 5 recommends that genetics move from standing-order authorization to an individual-physician order carrying a pre-test counseling acknowledgment. The physician-portal ordering flow enforces that acknowledgment today; the mobile standing-order rail does not yet, so the two paths currently authorize the same panel under different obligations.
Why an environment-wide record lives inside one patient's chart

The configuration is environment-wide, not patient-specific. The question it answers is not. A physician about to order for Ethan Park needs to know whether ordering for Ethan Park is authorized right now, and answering that from a global admin page means leaving the chart to find out why an order was refused. So the record is read here, beside Lab orders, and it is read only, because per the catalog's own contract the standing order and its test menu are a versioned, peer-reviewed, deploy-gated compliance artefact rather than mutable runtime state.

Being on the catalog with a configured test id is being authorized under the standing order. There is no second allowlist. Both routine blood labs and genetic kits resolve through the same OrderCatalog, so enforcement is identical on both paths.

What changed, and where it came from

fixed There was no way to log out, and no way to tell who was logged in. .prail__patient names the patient, so across 21 screens nothing said whose session this was and nothing ended it. v0 has both in its header, Dr. A. Okafor beside a Log out button, and the proposals dropped them when the header became a rail. A clinical surface that cannot say who is signed in cannot be handed to the next physician at the same workstation, which is the ordinary case in a clinic. The rail foot now carries the actor and the way out, under a rule, at the bottom where the least frequent act belongs. Proposed in section B as a domain.chart-rail addition, using the foot's existing exemption for a non-section action rather than claiming a new one.

fixed As bare underlined text the control was 15px tall, for the one act that ends the session. A text link is the sanctioned shape where a filled control will not fit, per register O5's fallback, but the hit area still has to be real: the label keeps its size and the target grew to 32px, with the same pointer: coarse guard the row-density variant uses restoring the full 44px floor on touch. Measured 14.35:1 flight-deck and 5.34:1 day.

carried The entire screen, from v0/standing-orders.html: both authorization records, all four record fields, the authorized-menu table and the genetics advisory. v1 has no ordering surface at all, so it has no concept of what authorizes an order.

carried The four record fields in v0's own order: Standing order reference, Ordering physician NPI, Canvas practitioner id, Ordering. Their fail-closed and fail-soft consequences are carried too, and moved from a docblock into the row where the consequence applies.

carried The Model under review advisory, from v0/standing-orders.html. Still an advisory: words in advisory colour, never a tile, so the real fail-closed hazard above outranks it by construction.

new The rollup counts anything that refuses an order, not just an incomplete record. v0's rollup counted records with no reference or no NPI; a kill switch that is off refuses just as hard and v0 rendered nothing for it, so the screen could read as fully authorized while every genetic order was being declined.

new Fail closed and fail soft are named as different things. v0 prints Not set, blocks ordering and Not set, writes fail soft in the same visual weight, which reads as two versions of the same problem.

dropped v0 elevates both cards to Tier 1. Two elevated cards on one screen spends the whole emphasis budget twice and leaves nothing to distinguish the authorized record from the one that is refusing. Blood keeps the elevation; genetics is flat and carries the advisory instead.

dropped v0's ds-pill status chips on every menu row. Fourteen identical Orderable pills is noise; the two rows that differ are the only ones worth a treatment, and they get No test id configured in place of a value.
