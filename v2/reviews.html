Why nothing on this screen has an edit control

Role and Release Matrix v1 section 2 gives the physician Read relevant patient on the audit log, and marks the log itself immutable append-only. That is not a permissions nicety: a signature that can be edited after the fact is not a signature. A correction to a released plan is a new package with its own hash, its own timestamp and its own row, and the superseded row stays exactly where it was. There is no delete and there is no amend.

The service worker appends; nobody edits. A row you disagree with is answered with another row, which is why the fifth row here records a failure that produced no package at all rather than quietly disappearing.

What changed, and where it came from

fixed There was no way to log out, and no way to tell who was logged in. .prail__patient names the patient, so across 21 screens nothing said whose session this was and nothing ended it. v0 has both in its header, Dr. A. Okafor beside a Log out button, and the proposals dropped them when the header became a rail. A clinical surface that cannot say who is signed in cannot be handed to the next physician at the same workstation, which is the ordinary case in a clinic. The rail foot now carries the actor and the way out, under a rule, at the bottom where the least frequent act belongs. Proposed in section B as a domain.chart-rail addition, using the foot's existing exemption for a non-section action rather than claiming a new one.

fixed As bare underlined text the control was 15px tall, for the one act that ends the session. A text link is the sanctioned shape where a filled control will not fit, per register O5's fallback, but the hit area still has to be real: the label keeps its size and the target grew to 32px, with the same pointer: coarse guard the row-density variant uses restoring the full 44px floor on touch. Measured 14.35:1 flight-deck and 5.34:1 day.

new The audit slices filter, and the counts are badges, so this surface reads like the inbox rather than like a different product. Each row carries the slice its own signed and released cells put it in, derived rather than restated, so a row cannot land in a slice its own cells contradict. Arrow keys move between tabs and aria-selected is the source of truth.

fixed No overlap, and the counts were right: a row was wrong. The tabs claimed 37 plus 3 plus 1 equals 41, and the fixture held a seventh package that fitted none of the three slices, so the arithmetic looked broken. It was not. This screen's own scope is every package you signed and every package you released, one row per release package, and that row read not signed, not released, no preview generated, no package emitted. There was no package and no act, so it was a row about the absence of a row. Removed; the counts stand. Ethan Park's review-pending state is the inbox's job, and he is in it.

new Priya Raghunathan's blocked row stays, and the distinction is the point. It also emits no package, but an act was attempted and refused, and the refusal with its reason is exactly what an audit is for. No act at all is not an audit entry. The filter keeps its draft branch as a guard: if a package with no act is ever added again, the note names it rather than the row disappearing into All acts.

carried This screen is why the inbox has no Released tab. The inbox had one, showing 41 patients whose plan had gone out, which is a record rather than work, and a thinner record than this: a row here is one release package, so a patient appears once per release rather than once, and it carries the hash, both timestamps and what the patient can see. The inbox tab was dropped and this is where that question is answered.

new The whole screen. Role and Release Matrix v1 section 2 grants the physician Audit log: Read relevant patient and neither v0 nor the reference surface offers anywhere to exercise it across patients.

carried The idea of a locked-record list, from v0/care-plan-note.html. v0's Notes History panel lists locked notes for one patient inside the care-plan screen. This keeps the record list and lifts it out of the chart, because an audit that only reads one chart at a time cannot answer "what did I release last week".

new Signed and Released are separate columns with separate timestamps and, on one row, one of them empty. v0 has a note lock and an order signature and no release act at all, so it has no way to show a plan that is signed but deliberately not yet visible to the patient.

new Failed validations get a row. The matrix lists six conditions under which release validation fails; a log that only records successes would hide every one of them.

gap The hash is rendered in the voice face with tabular figures and grouped in fours, because P4 retires the audit face from product surfaces. A hash is the one piece of content that argues hardest for mono, and it still does not get it. If reviewers find grouped voice figures unreadable at length, that is the finding to bring back.

dropped Any control that would let a physician remove a row, amend a signature or re-release the same package id. The log is append-only, so an edit affordance here would be a lie about what the backend permits.
